# DebAIter, an LLM-based Debating Platform
Our CS484 final project is an LLM-based debating platform. We aim to bring ease-of-use and convenience to formal debates while also providing serious debaters with the opportunity of solo practice thanks to our state-of-the-art large language models.

# Functionalities
Our application will allow any user to take on any one of the roles commonly seen in a normal debate setting. Including :

1. Affirmative Speaker, arguing in favor of a proposition
2. Negative Speaker, arguing against said proposition
3. Rebuttal Speaker, delivering counter-arguments in an optional rebuttal round
4. Cross-Examiner, in an optional policy debate setting
5. Judge, delivering the final decision and declaring the victor!

We allow you to take on any role on debates regarding any proposition, while we take care of everything else and be the timekeeper.

## Mix-and-Match
The users of our app will be able to take on any role mentioned above, or just let an LLM takeover. 

We also allow multiple users to join the same debate and pick and choose their own roles while filling the remaining open spots with LLMs. Any combination imaginable will be possible

## Timekeeping
Our application will automatically timekeep each speaker and will transcribe your argument thanks to our speech-to-text functionality. After your time is up, your speech will be recoreded and rendered to the screen.

## Final judgment
After all arguments have been made, a user or an LLM will deliver the final judgment and assign points to each camp. The camp with the highest points will win!

## Leaderboard
You'll be able to see where you stack up against all other debaters in our leaderboard. The more decisive your win against the other camp, the higher up on the leaderboard you'll be!
